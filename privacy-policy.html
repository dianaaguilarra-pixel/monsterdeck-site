MonsterDeck Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

1. Introduction

MonsterDeck ("we," "our," or "us") is a chore and reward app for families. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the MonsterDeck app, how we use it, who we share it with, and your choices. Parents or guardians create the account and enter all information about their children.

2. Who Uses MonsterDeck

Only parents or guardians create accounts and sign in. Children do not have accounts and never sign in.

3. Information We Collect

Parent or guardian:

  • Email address and password, used to create and sign in to your account. Passwords are stored in hashed form by our authentication provider.
  • Email addresses of adult family members you add, used for sign-in and to invite them to your family.

Family and children (entered by the parent):

  • Family name
  • Child profiles: first name or nickname, age, and avatar selection
  • Progress: stars, badges, weekly goals, chores, rewards, and reward goals

Device, app, and subscription information:

  • App version, build number, platform (iOS or Android), device OS version, and whether the app runs on a physical device or a simulator
  • Subscription status and an app user ID from RevenueCat

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never see or store your card details.

4. How We Use Information

We use this information to:

  • Create and secure your account, and send account emails such as verification and password reset
  • Sync your family's data across your devices
  • Show your subscription status and unlock premium features
  • Diagnose app and subscription problems
  • Comply with applicable law

We do not use personal information for advertising, and we do not use children's information for behavioral tracking. The app includes no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics SDKs.

5. Service Providers

We share information only with the service providers that run the app:

  • Supabase (cloud database and sign-in): stores your account, family, child profile, chore, reward, and app session records in the cloud
  • RevenueCat (purchases): manages subscriptions and receives your app user ID and purchase and entitlement information
  • Apple and Google: process payments and subscriptions under their own privacy policies

Supabase and RevenueCat may process IP addresses as part of running their services. We do not sell or rent personal information.

6. Data on Your Device

So the app keeps working offline, parts of your family's data are also stored on your device. Family details are kept in your device's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android). Deleting your account removes this local copy.

7. Parental Control

Parents control the family account. In the app you can edit or remove child profiles and adult members, clear this device's data with Reset Family Deck (this does not delete your cloud data), or delete your account.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Child profile removed: deleted from our database.
  • Account deleted (Profile, then Delete Account): we permanently delete your account, family, child profiles, chores, rewards, and app session records from our database, and clear this device.
  • Backups: our database provider keeps backups for a limited period under its standard schedule, after which they are deleted.
  • Subscriptions: deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings. Apple, Google, and RevenueCat keep purchase records under their own policies and as the law requires.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our database uses row-level security, so a family's data can be read and changed only by the parent account that owns it. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

We follow the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). A parent creates the account, verifies their email address, and gives consent during sign-up. We collect only the child information the parent enters to run the app: first name or nickname, age, avatar, and progress. We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, or other contact information from children, and we do not share children's information with third parties beyond the service providers in section 5. Parents can review, change, or delete a child's profile at any time, and can delete all family data by deleting the account.

11. Changes and Contact

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and notify you in the app. For privacy questions or requests, contact us at privacy@monsterdeck.app.
